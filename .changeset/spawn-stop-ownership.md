Spawn, stop and ownership. The core records who spawned which session (a new migration; ownership is set once, outlives presence, is transitive, and is kept while the session's transcript exists), marks archive and delete cascades as one batch undoable for 7 days, and records stop requests. `registerHost` / `currentHost` give each process one host registry on `globalThis[Symbol.for('kenning.host')]`, shared across copies of the module; `KenningHost.spawn` resolves `{sessionFile}` as soon as the session exists, and `stop(sessionFile, {by})` stops only its current turn. The CLI adds `kenning owned <target>` and `kenning stop <target>` (the human at a terminal only). The pi extension adds `session_spawn` (through the registered host, else a zellij pane or a detached headless `pi -p` woken later by its owner; optional worktree under `~/dev/worktrees/<repo>/<branch>` and fork) and `session_stop` (refused unless the caller owns the target). A spawned session's first prompt arrives as a `spawn` message from its owner, origin agent. `session_list`, `session_read` and `/kenning` show owners and owned sessions. An aborted run (a stop, or Esc) is no longer continued or re-woken by messages that were already pending.
